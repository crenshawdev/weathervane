<objective>
Make the Open-Meteo response → WeatherData transform and the JMA current-temp override
decision testable via two mechanical sync-helper extractions that mirror Phase 3's
`nws_alerts_from_response`/`bom_alerts_from_response` idiom (which mirrored
`air_quality_aqicn.rs::extract_aqi`), then prove the parse and the override decision with
fixture-based `#[test]` functions.

Purpose: `src/weather.rs` is currently near-zero covered because both the Open-Meteo parse
(the hourly/daily filter_map + CurrentWeather builder, lines ~144-208) and the JMA override
decision (lines 127-142) are trapped inside `async fn fetch_weather` alongside live HTTP.
Neither can be tested without extraction. This plan does the lowest-risk possible cut —
pulling the transform bodies into private sync fns with no behavior change — and covers
COV-02's two named paths: "Open-Meteo response → WeatherData parse" and "JMA current-temp
override path".

Per Phase 3's proven method (03-01-SUMMARY.md): baseline + post-plan `cargo llvm-cov`
measurements are recorded in the SUMMARY so the module-target improvement is a measured
delta rather than an assumption, and Plan 04-04 / Phase 5 (COV-07) have a real starting
datum for the workspace ≥70% gate.

Output: two private sync helpers, a new `#[cfg(test)] mod tests` block with 7-9 fixture-
based tests, and two measured llvm-cov numbers (baseline + post-plan) in the SUMMARY.
Wire contract stays byte-identical.
</objective>
